# {{leagueAbbrev}} Seasonal Agenda Items

Recurring board-meeting agenda items keyed by fiscal-year **phase** and **weekOfFY**
(FY week 0 = the week of Oct 1; the fiscal year runs Oct 1 – Sep 30). Use alongside
`board-meeting-agenda.md`: at each meeting, pull the items whose `weekOfFY` is at or
near the current week. `owning_role` is the board role that owns/presents the item.

Duplicate items observed across meetings within a phase are merged to their earliest
representative `weekOfFY`; the range where an item recurred is noted where relevant.

## Recurring / Year-Round Compliance Items

These obligations recur regardless of phase and should be tracked on every relevant agenda:

- **Abuse Awareness Training** resets **October 1**; all board members must complete it (badge required). — safety_officer
- **Constitution & By-laws** must be reviewed/updated and uploaded to Little League International. — secretary
- **Little League charter & insurance fees** — annual renewal (paid in Nov–Dec). — treasurer
- **Custom-jersey final registration deadline: 3/14, no exceptions**; teams finalized by 3/14. — uniform_coordinator / president
- **Junior & Senior Baseball commitment letters** — responses due Nov 30; roster published by 11/1. — vp_baseball
- **Sponsor thank-you tiers** — replica jersey for >$1,000 donors (~$75), dye-sub jersey for >$500 (~$36), signed thank-you card for >$100. — sponsorship_coordinator / president
