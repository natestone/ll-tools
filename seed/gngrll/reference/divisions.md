# {{leagueAbbrev}} Divisions Reference

{{leagueName}} ({{leagueAbbrev}}) offers baseball and softball divisions for players ages 4-16. The divisions listed below progress from the youngest introductory level up through the competitive teen divisions and tournament play. Age ranges are determined using the official Little League Baseball and Softball age charts. (source: website divisions/index)

## T-Ball (Rookie)

- **Age range:** Co-ed, ages 4-6 (the divisions overview page lists ages 4-7).
- **Format/rules highlights:** Introductory program created by Little League in 2013 to provide a foundation and introduction to baseball and softball grounded in fun, fitness, and fundamentals. Games and practices last about 1 hour, running from approximately April 29 - June 10.
- **Notable notes:** Players are required to play at least one season of T-Ball, but may play more, before moving on to the Coach Pitch division. See "Tee Ball (Rookie) Rules." (source: website divisions/t-ball)

## Coach Pitch (A)

- **Age range:** Co-ed, ages 5-6.
- **Format/rules highlights:** A Minor League team designed as a stepping stone from Tee Ball to a higher level of play. Players hit pitched balls, record outs, and score runs. Coaches do all of the pitching from a knee, while players work on the accuracy of their throws and fielding batted balls. Coaches are on the field providing instruction during the game. Games and practices last about 1 hour, running from approximately April 29 - June 10.
- **Notable notes:** See "Coach Pitch (A) Rules." Season begins as soon as weather permits following April break. (source: website divisions/coach-pitch-a)

## Player Pitch (AA)

- **Age range:** Ages 7-8. Softball and baseball division.
- **Format/rules highlights:** Intended to bridge the transition from coaches pitching to players pitching to each other. Coaches are on the field providing instruction during the game. Increased competition at this level; standings are kept and there is a two-game playoff at the end of the season to determine a Player Pitch champion. Games and practices last about 1.5-2 hours, running from approximately April 10 - June 14.
- **Notable notes:** Players need to attend Assessments. See separate softball (SB) and baseball (BB) rules for Player Pitch (AA). (source: website divisions/player-pitch-aa)

## Minors (AAA)

- **Age range:** Ages 9-10. Softball and baseball division.
- **Format/rules highlights:**
  - **Baseball:** For players whose skills have advanced beyond Player Pitch but who are not ready for the Major Division. Players do all of the pitching and coaches remain in the dugout during the game. Increased competition; standings are kept and there is a playoff at the end of the season to determine a Minors Division Champion.
  - **Softball:** Bridges the transition from coach-pitch to player-pitch. Players pitch in every odd inning (1, 3, 5) and coaches pitch in the even innings (2, 4, 6) or to give a batter the opportunity to hit. Coaches are on the field providing instruction. Standings are kept and there is a two-game playoff to determine a Minors Division Champion.
  - Games and practices last about 1.5-2 hours, running from approximately April 10 - June 14.
- **Notable notes:** Players in the Minor Division age 9 and above are eligible to be selected for all-star teams. Players need to attend Assessments. See separate softball (SB) and baseball (BB) rules for Minors (AAA). (source: website divisions/minors-aaa)

## Majors

- **Age range:** Ages 11-12. Softball and baseball division.
- **Format/rules highlights:** Intended for the most advanced players. Increased competition at this level; standings are kept and there is a playoff at the end of the season to determine the Major Division Champion. Games and practices last about 1.5-2.5 hours, running from approximately April 10 - June 14.
- **Notable notes:** Once a player has been placed on a Major team, they may not return to a lower division. Players in the Major Division are eligible for all-star teams. Players need to attend Assessments. See separate Majors Softball and Majors Baseball rules. (source: website divisions/majors)

## Juniors & Seniors

- **Age range:** Ages 13-16 (the divisions overview page lists the combined Juniors & Seniors range as 12-16). Softball and baseball divisions.
- **Format/rules highlights:**
  - **Softball:** Uses a conventional 60-foot diamond with a 43-foot pitching distance.
  - **Baseball:** Co-ed program using a conventional 90-foot diamond with a pitching distance of 60 feet, 6 inches. Modified diamond dimensions may be used during the regular season.
  - Games and practices start in June after the school season is over.
- **Notable notes:** Described as a tremendous value and a supplementary ball program for this age group. Players do NOT need to attend assessments. (source: website divisions/juniors-seniors)

## Senior Tournament Team

- **Age range:** Ages 15-16 by Little League age rules.
- **Format/rules highlights:** The Senior Little League International Tournament is a community-team style tournament allowing two towns to combine to create a team. For the 2025 tournament, {{leagueName}} combined with Portland LL, with each league providing 7 players (coaches select 7 if more sign up; a tryout may be held). No regular season play is required. Lineups are 9 batters as selected by the coaching staff; there is no guaranteed playing time, and non-starters may or may not see playing time.
- **Notable notes:** Players must have played Little League for Portland LL or {{leagueName}} within the last 4 years. 3-6 practices are held on weekday evenings in June/July for assessment; players must attend at least 3 practices to be eligible for on-field play. Signup deadline was March 21 (player requirements also cite May 1 signup, final confirmation May 15). Tournament path: State Tournament in Bangor, ME (July 8-11), Senior LL East Regional in Bangor, ME (July 15-21), and World Series in Easley, South Carolina (July 26 - August 2) if the regional is won. Coached by Nate Stone, Brian Bechard, and additional coaches from {{townA}} and Portland. (source: website divisions/juniors-seniors/senior-tournament-team)
