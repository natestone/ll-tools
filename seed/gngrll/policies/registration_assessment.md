# Registration & Assessment

This section governs how players register with {{communities}} Little
League ({{leagueAbbrev}}), how league age and division placement are determined, how player
assessments (evaluations) are run, and how teams are formed. The league website is
the authoritative source of current policy; board agendas and league communications
provide procedural detail and history.

## Divisions & League Age

{{leagueAbbrev}} offers divisions for all players ages 4-16 (source: website divisions).
The divisions and their age ranges are:

- T-Ball, co-ed, ages 4-6 (source: website register-practices-assessments)
- Coach Pitch A, co-ed, ages 5-6 (source: website register-practices-assessments)
- Player Pitch AA, softball and baseball, ages 7-8 (source: website register-practices-assessments)
- Minors AAA, softball and baseball, ages 9-10 (source: website register-practices-assessments)
- Majors, softball and baseball, ages 11-12 (source: website register-practices-assessments)
- Juniors & Seniors, softball and baseball, ages 13-16 (source: website register-practices-assessments)

A player's division is set by their Little League "League Age," not their calendar
age. The Age Determination Date is the child's actual age as of August 31 of the
current year for baseball, and January 1 of the current year for softball (source:
website frequently-asked-questions other-faqs). Parents must determine league age
from the official Little League Baseball and Softball Age Charts before registering
(source: website divisions; source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open - Winter
Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger & Sandlot!").

The minimum age to register is 4, for Tee Ball baseball (source: website
frequently-asked-questions other-faqs). Older children who have never played may
join; for safety they may be asked to play in Minors unless they are League Age 12
(source: website frequently-asked-questions other-faqs).

## How to Register

All registration is completed online at {{leagueWebsite}} (source:
website register-practices-assessments). To complete registration a player must
also print and complete a Medical Release Form, which is required to participate
(source: website register-practices-assessments). A Medical Release Form must be
completed each year for every player to keep information current (source: website
guidelines-documents annual-requirements). Parents who have not submitted a medical
release form may bring a printed copy to assessments (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder").

**New parents** create an account, enter parent/guardian information, add each
participant through the registration wizard, and complete uniform selection,
volunteer options, and payment (source: website new-registrations). **Returning
parents** log in to their existing account, select "Register Now" for each returning
child, and may add additional participants during the process (source: website
returning-registrations). Payment may be made online or by check at a later date by
selecting the "Check" payment option (source: website new-registrations; source:
website returning-registrations).

Questions about registration should be directed to the {{leagueAbbrev}} Information Officer at
{{infoEmail}} (source: website register-practices-assessments).

## Residency, Documents & Eligibility

Little League no longer requires proof of residency for players League Age 7 and
under, and a player who stays with the league continuously from age 7 on never needs
to prove residency (source: website age-7-under-registration). For these players,
parents enter their address (or check "Same as Parent") and select one of the
league's schools to bypass the system's residency restrictions (source: website
age-7-under-registration).

To get through registration, parents should skip the document uploads (except a
driver's license if volunteering); proof-of-residency documents are not needed at
registration and are only requested if the player later makes an All-Star team
(source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Tips - Skip the Documents!"). Players League
Age 8 and above must meet residency requirements to be eligible for All-Stars, but
still complete registration by selecting a school and uploading no documents (source:
email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Tips - Skip the Documents!"). A player who is 8 this
year but played as a 7-year-old in the league last year meets the continuous-play
rule and may play (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Tips - Skip the Documents!").
Families of players League Age 9 or older who do not live in {{townA}}, {{townB}}, or
{{townC}} should contact the league for eligibility clarification (source: email
"{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Tips - Skip the Documents!").

All-Star documentation requirements (submitted only if a player makes a team) include
an All-Star Letter of Intent due by May 1, a signed School Enrollment Form, and a
Birth Certificate; {{leagueAbbrev}} does not retain these documents between seasons and submits
all documentation to Little League District Officers (source: website
guidelines-documents annual-requirements).

## Registration Timeline & Deadlines

Registration opens in the winter and remains open through the season. For 2026,
registration opened January 17 for all divisions from Tee Ball through Seniors
(source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open - Winter Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger
& Sandlot!"). For 2025, regular-season registration opened January 15 and winter
clinic registration opened January 4 (source: agenda 2025-01-27).

There is a firm final registration deadline to guarantee a customized jersey; in
2025 this deadline was March 14 with "no exceptions," and teams were finalized by
that date (source: agenda 2025-01-27; source: agenda 2025-02-10). In 2024 the
customized-jersey deadline was March 9, with the draft the following day on March 10
(source: agenda 2024-01-21; source: agenda 2024-02-18).

The league accepts players who register late, or even after the season begins, but
late registrations may not receive uniforms on time (source: agenda 2021-03-02). Late
or post-assessment registrants can still be placed on a team (source: email
"Registration").

## Assessments (Player Evaluations)

Assessments are required for all baseball and softball players League Age 7 and up so
the league can prepare for team creation (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open
- Winter Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger & Sandlot!"; source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder"). Players in Tee Ball and Coach Pitch (ages 4-6)
do not attend the AA/AAA/Majors assessments.

**Format.** Assessments are run as station-based evaluations rather than a single
tryout. Stations typically include ground balls, fly/pop balls, tee work, soft toss,
and throwing, each staffed by a coach and an assessor and supported by high school
player volunteers (source: agenda 2022-02-13; source: agenda 2023-02-05). Baseball
and softball are assessed on separate days, and players are grouped into time blocks
by division/age (source: agenda 2022-02-13; source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments &
Registration Reminder"). For 2026, baseball was scheduled Monday Feb 23 and softball
Wednesday Feb 25 at the {{indoorVenue}} Gym, with AA (ages 7-8) at 5:30-6:30pm, AAA
(ages 9-10) at 6:30-7:30pm, and Majors (ages 11-12) at 7:30-8:30pm (source: email
"{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder").

**What to bring.** Players should bring a change of shoes to protect the gym floor
and their glove; helmets and bats are optional because the league provides them on
site (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder"; source: email
"{{leagueAbbrev}} - Reminder Baseball Assessments"). During assessments, parents meet
separately (in the library or chorus room) for a presentation on the season (source:
email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder"; source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Reminder Baseball Assessments").

**Rain/make-up.** A make-up date is reserved in case a session is cancelled for
weather or school closure. In 2026 Friday Feb 27 was held as the rain/make-up date,
and baseball assessments were in fact postponed from Feb 23 to Feb 27 due to a school
closure (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Assessments & Registration Reminder"; source: email
"{{leagueAbbrev}} - Baseball Assessments Postponed To Friday 2/27").

## Team Formation & the Draft

After assessments, teams are formed by a coaches' draft. The draft is typically held
a few days after assessments (in 2024 and 2025 planning it followed as "Draft 3/10")
(source: agenda 2024-01-21; source: agenda 2025-01-27). Ratings for players who miss
assessments are chosen by the board/coaches before the draft (source: agenda
2022-03-09). The draft is run division by division among that division's volunteer
head coaches and takes roughly 30-45 minutes; for example, the 2026 AA draft was
organized among the five AA head coaches (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - AA Draft"). After
the draft, coaches load their rosters into GameChanger from the {{registrationPlatform}}
registration system (source: email "Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - AA Draft").

## Playing Up (Division Placement Above Age Level)

{{leagueAbbrev}} rarely allows a player to move up a level, because keeping players with their
age peers is held to produce quality play at every level and better development,
leadership opportunities, and meaningful repetitions for the player (source: website
frequently-asked-questions playing-up). On the softball side the same philosophy
applies but with more room for movement between levels to ensure teams exist at all
levels (source: website frequently-asked-questions playing-up).

The board's operative play-up rule is: the league sticks with the Little League age
charts as established, and play-up is handled case by case, subject to the safety of
the player and other players, an assessment of the player, and the requirement that
the player fall within the range of competency of others at that level; grade level
is also considered (source: agenda 2024-02-04). If a player is approved to play up,
that approval may continue to the next levels as well (source: agenda 2024-02-04).
For siblings, a younger sibling may play up only if they are moving up just one year
in age, again subject to safety and assessment (source: agenda 2024-02-04). This
policy was adopted by motion (Mike Amergian, seconded by Amanda Buxton, carried
unopposed) with direction to reflect both the case-by-case and sibling scenarios in
the bylaws (source: agenda 2024-02-04).

to get work in before assessments (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open - Winter
Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger & Sandlot!").

## Sandlot & Challenger Registration

Players in AAA or Majors baseball who want to play Sandlot Saturdays register for
that division in addition to the regular season; registering signals the Sandlot VP
to build squads in GameChanger (source: email "{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open - Winter
Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger & Sandlot!"). Sandlot games are played primarily
on weekends, use umpires, are played like regular competition games, and reshuffle
teams so non-travel players get more playing time and additional positions (source:
website frequently-asked-questions what-are-sandlot-games). The Challenger division
is a unified program for players ages 4-18 pairing Little League players with players
requiring special accommodations; it is also available at registration (source: email
"{{leagueAbbrev}} - Registration Open - Winter Clinics, Spring Season, Challenger & Sandlot!").
