# Fields & Capital Projects

This policy governs how {{leagueName}} maintains
its playing fields, manages capital projects and land, and authorizes related
spending. The league's published Field Directory and Rainout & Lightning
Guidelines are the current source of truth for day-to-day field use; board
agendas and league correspondence establish the procedures and history for
capital work and expenditure authorization.

## 1. Field Inventory & Ownership

{{leagueAbbrev}} plays on a mix of league-owned, town-owned, and school fields across its
three communities (source: website field-directory). The published field
directory covers Douglas Field ({{townA}}), Penney Field ({{townB}}), Pennell
Field ({{townA}}), Newbegin Field ({{townA}}), Intervale Field ({{townA}}/{{townB}}),
Russell School Field, Dunn School Field, St. Gregory Fields, Rowe Station/NG Rec
Fields, Memorial Field ({{townB}}), and the {{townC}} fields at Mill Street,
Jordan Small Middle School, and {{townC}} Elementary (source: website
field-directory).

Ownership and level assignments are recorded per field:

- **Penney Field** is owned by {{leagueName}} and hosts Minors (AAA) and
  Majors (source: website field-directory penney-field).
- **Pennell Field** and **Newbegin Field** are owned by the Town of {{townA}}.
  Pennell hosts AAA Softball, Majors Softball, and Majors 50/70 Baseball;
  Newbegin is the Coach Pitch (co-ed) field south of the basketball court
  (source: website field-directory pennell-field; source: website
  field-directory newbegin-field).
- **Intervale Field** (540 Intervale Road, {{townB}}) hosts Minors (AAA)
  and Majors, with parking in the field behind the outfield fence (source:
  website field-directory intervale-field).

Because several fields are owned by others (Town of {{townA}}, schools, the district),
{{leagueAbbrev}} must coordinate access and stay in line with the property owner's
requirements. The league escalates access disputes to the Town when needed (for
example, the 2023 Town of {{townA}} access issue that required mediation between the
league, the Town, and Will Boyle) (source: agenda 2023-03-14).

## 2. Field Use & Etiquette

The **home team is responsible for field maintenance at every game**; visiting
teams are welcome to pitch in (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Snack Shacks & Field
Maintenance). After each game and practice, fields must be **dragged** to keep
weeds out of the infield and help the field shed rain and weather; any fan,
parent, or player may grab a rake or drag to smooth the infield (source: email
{{leagueAbbrev}} - Snack Shacks & Field Maintenance).

Where {{leagueAbbrev}} shares a site with another program, the league must respect that
program's needs. At Newbegin and Pennell in {{townA}}, weekday events overlap with
{{townA}} Rec pickup, so start times and arrival are controlled: **Tee Ball and Coach
Pitch events start at 6:00 pm at Newbegin, softball games start at 6:15 pm, no
players or families may arrive before 5:30 pm, and warm-ups run 5:45-6:10 pm**
(source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Newbegin & Pennell Field Etiquette and Start Times).

Fields with shared or locked facilities have specific closing procedures. At
St. Gregory's, teams practicing on the third field share equipment and use the
outfield of the Back Field closest to the parking lot, and the porta potty must
be locked at the end of the evening (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Combinations to field
bins).

## 3. Equipment Bins & Security

Field gear is stored in bins/sheds on site. **Bin and lock combinations are for
adults only and must not be shared**, because the league has experienced theft in
prior years (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Combinations to field bins). The board has
noted repeated theft of baseballs from Douglas and considered mitigations
including relocating bins, better bins, or moving equipment into the snack shack
if problems continue (source: agenda 2025-01-12). Volunteers periodically
assemble and stock the field gear bins before opening week (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Equipment bins).

## 4. Rainout, Weather & Lightning

The league's goal is always to get as much safe and fun play as possible; the
published Rainout & Lightning Guidelines are the operative standard (source:
website frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).

- **Cancellations run through GameChanger.** Coaches cancel practices and games
  by editing and canceling the event in the app; for games, coaches must
  coordinate with the opposing coach before canceling (source: website
  frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).
- **Timing of cancellations.** When there is only a threat of rain (no rain yet /
  forecast only), do not cancel until **one hour before** game or practice time,
  so you can see whether the weather will miss the fields. Tee Ball and Coach
  Pitch may cancel earlier because of their short duration (source: website
  frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).
- **Temperature guidance.** For Tee Ball and Coach Pitch, if it is above 45
  degrees and not windy, give it a shot; below 45 and drizzly or windy it likely
  won't be fun. For AA (player pitch), AAA (Minors), and Majors, give it a shot
  at 40 degrees and above; play through drizzle when there is no lightning or
  thunder, and bring towels to dry off baseballs (source: website
  frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).
- **Field-protection standard.** If the field is soaked with standing water, cancel.
  If you leave deep mud-prints when walking on it, the field is not safe and can be
  damaged, so do not play. **{{townC}}'s Mill Street field drains poorly** and often
  takes 12-24 hours to dry after a heavy rain (source: website
  frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).
- **Lightning policy.** Follow Little League's rule: "If you hear it - clear it"
  (clear the field for at least 30 minutes) and "If you see it - flee it" (leave
  the field / game ends). When lightning is within 10 miles, suspend play
  immediately and clear all dugouts and field areas. An "all clear" may be issued
  only when all lightning/thunder has moved beyond the 10-mile radius with none
  expected, or **at least 30 minutes has elapsed** since the last visible strike
  or thunder (source: website frequently-asked-questions rainout-lightning-guidelines).

## 5. Seasonal Field Maintenance

{{leagueAbbrev}} runs organized field cleanup and preparation days before and during the
season. The season calendar sets a primary **Field Cleanup day with a rain-date
backup** (for example, 4/13 with a 4/27 rain date in 2025, and 4/12 with a 4/26
rain date in 2026) (source: agenda 2025-01-12; source: agenda 2026-01-12).
Cleanup work focuses on raking leaves, picking up trash, and working the infield
surfaces so base paths are clear of weeds, rocks, and large holes (source: email
{{leagueAbbrev}} - Field Clean Up Day).

The league also organizes short-notice grounds work when materials arrive or
playoffs approach, such as re-cutting infield lines and adding infield mix at
Douglas and Russell fields, using volunteer crews with lawn tractors, trailers,
shovels, and rakes (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Short Notice Field Retrofit). Routine
fall maintenance such as fertilizer for Douglas and Penney is approved by board
motion (source: agenda 2023-03-14).

## 6. Snack Shacks

Operating the snack shacks supports the fan experience and league finances.
Coverage is scheduled via signup links; when a shack is closed during a time it
should be open, volunteers are asked to open it (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Snack
Shacks & Field Maintenance). Operational questions go to the designated
concessions leads for softball and baseball (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Snack Shacks
& Field Maintenance).

## 7. Capital Projects & Land

{{leagueAbbrev}} undertakes multi-year capital projects to build and improve fields. Major
initiatives are approved and tracked by the board.

- **Intervale Fields & Recreation Area.** In partnership with the Royal River
  Conservation Trust ({{landTrust}}), this project creates new ballfields while helping
  conserve over 180 acres in {{townB}} for outdoor recreation (source: email
  Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} Intervale Fields Fundraiser Launch). Funding has combined an {{landTrust}}
  budget, a GoFundMe fundraiser, and a grant. The board authorized purchasing
  Intervale materials and equipment funded by **$35K from the SK Grant plus $8K
  from GoFundMe ({{amount}}K total)**, contingent on confirmation that groundwork would
  be done in time for storage (source: agenda 2024-01-07). {{landTrust}} budget figures and
  final contributions are tracked meeting to meeting (for example, a confirmed
  35K {{landTrust}} budget in early 2023 and a final {{landTrust}} payment of {{amount}} noted in early
  2025) (source: agenda 2023-03-14; source: agenda 2025-01-12). Project phases
  have included tree removal, dugout pads, fencing, benches, and containers on
  site (source: agenda 2025-01-12).
- **Pennway Park @ Morrison Field (Penney Road).** In 2023 the league built a
  one-third-scale replica of Fenway Park's Green Monster at Penney/Morrison Field,
  including clearing tree line, setting 16-foot posts five feet deep in 20-inch
  sonotubes, converting the infield to a multi-purpose softball/baseball diamond
  with 50+ yards of infield mix, and mounting 30 printed panels for the wall face
  (source: website projects pennway-morrison-field). The board confirmed the
  Fenway project for Penney Field and coordinated with the funders/sponsors before
  proceeding (source: agenda 2023-03-14).
- **Bennett Road parcels.** {{leagueAbbrev}} holds land at Bennett Road transferred from
  {{state}} DOT; because the pit predates 1970 it is grandfathered and DOT had no
  obligation to reclaim it before transfer. The league sources clean, inert fill
  from area contractors to level and develop the site (source: email Re: Bennett
  Road). Leveling and fill progress is tracked by the board (source: agenda
  2024-01-21; source: agenda 2022-11-14).
- **Field conversions.** Converting a field to a larger 50/70 layout is a capital
  action approved by motion; the board approved **{{amount}} to convert Penney to
  50/70** (source: agenda 2023-03-14).

New fencing installations (such as Intervale) are organized as volunteer work
days, calling for 10-12 adults with gloves and power tools, and proceed rain or
shine once the poles are set (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Intervale Fence Installation).

## 8. Fundraising & Grants for Capital Work

Capital projects are funded through a combination of grants ({{landTrust}}, SK Grant,
Alfond), sponsorships/banners, and public fundraisers. The Intervale fundraiser
was launched publicly via GoFundMe alongside the {{landTrust}} partnership (source: email
Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} Intervale Fields Fundraiser Launch). Outfield-fence banner ("Fenway
Fence Screen") sponsorships have been used to fund field structures, raising
several thousand dollars against installation and engineering costs (source:
agenda 2021-05-02). Structural engineering for the Fenway wall was performed by
outside firms (SW Cole and Tim Shelley) (source: agenda 2021-05-02).

## 9. Spending Authorization

Field, equipment, and capital expenditures are approved by board motion, second,
and vote at a board meeting, and are recorded in the minutes (source: agenda
2023-03-14). Motions typically authorize a specific dollar cap or a specific
purchase. Examples of the practice include:

- "Motion by Amanda Buxton to spend up to {{amount}} on equipment for the 2023
  season" (source: agenda 2023-03-14).
- "Move to purchase up to {{amount}} in fertilizer" (source: agenda 2023-03-14).
- "Motion for {{amount}} to convert Penney to 50/70" (source: agenda 2023-03-14).
- Motion to purchase Intervale materials/equipment funded by grant and GoFundMe,
  contingent on groundwork timing (source: agenda 2024-01-07).

Motions carry by majority vote and may be contested (for example, a uniforms
motion capped at {{amount}} that passed 7-2), so a simple majority of voting board
members present is sufficient unless a larger threshold is otherwise required
(source: agenda 2021-05-02). Larger land and contract matters (property sales,
extensions, grant budgets) are likewise handled by board motion (source: agenda
2022-01-22).

## 10. Property Taxes & Compliance

As a landholding league, {{leagueAbbrev}} must maintain its tax and charter status and
manage property obligations on its parcels. The board tracks tax-exempt and
state charter status and pays property taxes on its {{townB}} holdings
(Intervale, Penney Road, and two Bennett parcels), verifying each September that
taxes were properly applied (source: agenda 2025-11-24).
