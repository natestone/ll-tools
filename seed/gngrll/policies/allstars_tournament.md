# All-Stars & Tournament Policies & Procedures

This section governs how {{leagueName}} ({{leagueAbbrev}}) determines
interest, selects players and coaches, establishes eligibility, and fields teams
for Little League All-Star and District/State/Regional tournament play. The
league's published All-Stars & Tournaments FAQ is the authoritative current
source of truth; board agendas and league emails supply operating detail and
historical practice.

## 1. Eligibility & Commitment (Letter of Intent)

Each year the league determines interest in All-Star play by asking each player's
parents/guardians to submit a signed All-Star Letter of Intent (also referred to
as the All-Star Commitment Online Form) (source: website all-stars-tournaments).
All-Stars are open to baseball and softball players ages 9-16 as indicated by the
Little League Age Charts for Baseball and Softball (source: website
all-stars-tournaments).

To be eligible for consideration, the family must submit the All-Star Commitment
Online Form by 11:59 PM on May 19th; players without a submitted form cannot be
considered for tournament play (source: website all-stars-tournaments; source:
email {{leagueAbbrev}} - All Star Commitment Form - Online Form - Due Monday, May 18).
Completed paper forms may be submitted to {{infoEmail}} (source:
website all-stars-tournaments). The Letter-of-Intent deadline is set annually on
the league calendar; recent seasons have used early-to-mid May (e.g., "All star
letters due - 5/6" in 2023-24, "5/12" in 2025, and "5/10" in 2026) (source:
agenda 2023-05-07; source: agenda 2025-05-05; source: agenda 2026-05-18).

Once committed to All-Stars, players must be available to attend all games
regardless of vacations or other conflicts. Parents must commit to the entire
All-Star schedule, which can run from June 1 through August 24 depending on how
far the team advances. Players who fail to attend for non-medical reasons will be
replaced on the team (source: website all-stars-tournaments). Attendance and
forfeiture are treated as league-critical: if the league forfeits a game due to
non-attendance, Little League can refuse to charter {{leagueAbbrev}} in the future (source:
website all-stars-tournaments).

## 2. Residency & Documentation

If a child has been enrolled as a student of MSAD 15 or RSU 14 prior to October 1
of the previous calendar year, the school district can provide school-enrollment
information on the family's behalf to prove residency and eligibility; permission
for the district to provide this information is granted through the Letter of
Intent / Commitment Form (source: website all-stars-tournaments; source: email
{{leagueAbbrev}} - All Star Commitment Form - Online Form - Due Monday, May 18). Families do
not need to obtain the enrollment form themselves; the league has the school
districts complete them together (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - All Star Commitment Form
- Online Form - Due Monday, May 18).

If the child is NOT an MSAD 15 or RSU 14 student, the family must provide
additional documentation to prove residency, and only needs to provide these
documents if the child is selected to an All-Star team. Selections are made by
June 1, and the documents must be presented to the team coach by June 3 (source:
website all-stars-tournaments). Eligibility and the required documents follow
Little League's published International Tournament / League Eligibility
requirements (three documents required) (source: website all-stars-tournaments).
All eligibility questions should be directed to the child's team manager or to the
league secretary at {{secretaryEmail}} (source: website
all-stars-tournaments).

## 3. Team Composition & Levels

The league fields All-Star teams at the standard Little League tournament levels
(for example 9-10, 10-11/11-12, and Majors), and the specific levels fielded each
year are decided by the board based on participation (source: agenda 2022-05-19;
source: agenda 2024-05-21). Each All-Star team is composed of a minimum of 12 and
a maximum of 14 players (source: website all-stars-tournaments; source: email
{{leagueAbbrev}} - Age 9-10 All Star Voting & Coaches).

The number and structure of teams is set by board motion each season. For example,
the board approved fielding two 9-10 teams and one 11-12 team for Baseball in 2024,
pending confirmation that an A/B construction (versus a balanced build) did not
violate Little League rules (source: agenda 2024-05-21).

Beyond the standard All-Star teams, the league also offers supplemental
opportunities when the talent pool is deep, including a Mid-Majors (9-11) team and
{{state}} {{district}} "Rookies" (8-10) tournament teams for players who did not make the
9-10 All-Star team (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Rookies All Stars 8-10 & Mid-Majors
9-11 All Stars Signup; source: agenda 2026-05-18). These supplemental teams are
opened to all players Little League ages 8 through 11 who wish to play; if more
than 12 players sign up, the coaches select the team from those who signed up
(source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Rookies All Stars 8-10 & Mid-Majors 9-11 All Stars
Signup). Opening and funding of these teams is contingent on sufficient
participation and is authorized by board motion (source: agenda 2025-05-19).

## 4. Selection Process (Coaches' Ranked Vote)

The league strives for a straightforward selection process. Regular-season coaches
in each level cast a ranked vote of the players in their level, voting for the
strongest and best players and ranking every player who signed up and is eligible
(source: website all-stars-tournaments). Points are assigned based on the
rankings, and the top players are selected by point total (source: website
all-stars-tournaments). By board motion, selection uses a ranked vote of 1-14 with
a point tally inversely correlated to rank (i.e., a #1 ranking earns 14 points)
(source: agenda 2025-05-19).

The league is required to take a minimum of 12 and a maximum of 14 players at each
level. Where a tie exists (for example, players 12-16 all having the same number of
votes), the board of directors holds a discussion with the selected All-Star head
coach and trims the team to the appropriate size (source: website
all-stars-tournaments). The league's goal is to field the best team possible
regardless of age; 12-year-olds are not prioritized over 11-year-olds, and
tiebreakers are decided by the board (source: website all-stars-tournaments).

Operationally, coaches submit their selections/ballots individually to the league,
the board confirms the final roster size (12-14) at a board meeting, and the teams
are announced by June 1 (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Age 9-10 All Star Voting &
Coaches; source: website all-stars-tournaments). Rosters are approved by board
motion (for example, the 2025 Majors, AAA Baseball, and AAA Softball teams were
each accepted by unanimous board vote) (source: agenda 2025-05-19). Player
selection and uniform-ordering deadlines are set annually; for 2026 the softball
selection ballot ran 5/25-5/27 (with 6/1 needed for uniforms) and the baseball
ballot ran 5/30-6/1 (with 6/5 needed for uniforms) (source: agenda 2026-05-18).
Late signups do not entitle a player to an exception (source: agenda 2026-06-01).

## 5. Coach Selection

Coaches interested in managing or assisting an All-Star team indicate their
interest (for example via a signup sent to the coach email or when submitting their
selection ballot) (source: agenda 2026-05-18; source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Age 9-10 All
Star Voting & Coaches). All-Star managers and coaches are selected/approved by the
board, by vote where needed (for example, the 2026 AAA Baseball manager was
approved 5-4 and the Majors manager was approved unanimously) (source: agenda
2026-06-01; source: agenda 2025-05-19).

## 6. Rules of Play (Tournament)

All-Star tournament rules of play differ significantly from the regular Little
League season (source: website all-stars-tournaments). Key differences include:

- Continuous batting order (CBR): all players selected to the team and present at
  the game are placed in the lineup and are eligible to bat (source: website
  all-stars-tournaments).
- Players batting in the 10-14 spots may not see any at-bats due to the 15-run
  rule after 3 innings; players in the 13-14 spots may not see any at-bats if a
  game is lost under the 10-run rule after 4 innings (source: website
  all-stars-tournaments).
- Players are not guaranteed any time in the field; families should understand
  and explain this to their player in advance (source: website
  all-stars-tournaments).

When {{leagueAbbrev}} teams play any non-{{leagueAbbrev}} team (as in tournament play), {{state}} {{district}} interleague rules apply; {{leagueAbbrev}}'s own rules apply only when a {{leagueAbbrev}} team plays
another {{leagueAbbrev}} team (source: agenda 2026-05-13). Pitch-count and rest requirements
must be tracked (scored via GameChanger for applicable levels), all pitchers must
report pitch counts from other leagues, and pitching in any game (internal or
travel) counts toward pitch counts; teams found in violation forfeit the game in
which ineligible players pitched (source: website playoffs-2026; source: website
playoffs-2025).

## 7. Tournament Structure, Dates & Advancement

The league participates in the {{state}} {{district}} tournament structure, which can
advance to State and Regional levels (source: agenda 2026-05-13; source: website
all-stars-tournaments). Representative 2026 schedule/advancement dates include:
{{district}} AAA and Majors starting June 15 (weekdays only, ending about July 3),
State AAA hosted by Five Town Little League in Camden, ME July 10-16, State Majors
in Westbrook July 7-12, Regional AAA in Staten Island, NY July 24-29, and Regional
Majors in Bristol, CT July 18-23 (source: agenda 2026-05-13). Brackets are posted
on the {{state}} {{district}} website (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Congratulations to our All
Stars). All-Star game start windows are set annually on the league calendar; recent
seasons list softball All-Stars beginning mid-June and baseball All-Stars a few
days later (for example softball 6/17 and baseball 6/24 in 2023-24; softball 6/13
and baseball 6/20 in 2025; softball 6/12 and baseball 6/19 in 2026) (source:
agenda 2023-05-07; source: agenda 2025-05-05; source: agenda 2026-05-18). The
website notes games start June 16th for softball and June 20th for baseball, with
teams generally eliminated by early July but with the possibility of advancing to
state and regional levels (source: website all-stars-tournaments).

## 8. Tournament Day Operations

Each All-Star team must provide a helper who reports to the tournament director 90
minutes before game time and is released at first pitch; this helper cannot be a
coach (source: agenda 2026-05-18; source: agenda 2026-06-01). All-Star team books
(paperwork) are due by the deadline set each season (for example, June 10 in 2026)
(source: agenda 2026-05-18; source: agenda 2026-06-01). When {{leagueAbbrev}} considers
hosting a tournament, that decision is made by the board weighing benefits (such
as snack-shack revenue) against the workload involved (source: agenda 2026-05-18;
source: agenda 2025-05-05).

## 9. Uniforms

The league orders All-Star uniforms/jerseys and hats for tournament players
(source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Rookies All Stars 8-10 & Mid-Majors 9-11 All Stars
Signup). Because custom uniforms may not arrive before All-Stars begin, the board
has authorized ordering a set of generic uniform jerseys to have on hand; if issued,
the team is responsible for collecting and returning them so they can be reused,
and the generics are returned once the custom uniforms arrive (source: agenda
2025-05-05; source: agenda 2026-05-13; source: agenda 2026-06-01).

## 10. Advancement Costs (State/Regional)

Advancing to State and Regional tournaments is a significant travel and financial
commitment. For deep runs (such as the New England Regional in Bristol, CT), the
league's goal is to provide transportation reimbursement and hotel stays for
players and coaches, and the league fundraises to cover driving, meals, and hotel
costs (source: website all-stars-tournaments; source: email Money for New England
Regional Tournament). The league also coordinates with the {{state}} District/State
administrators on tournament scheduling to preserve adequate pitching rest before
advancement (for example requesting a later state-tournament start to allow four
days' rest before the regional) (source: email State Tournaments).

## 11. Conduct at Tournament & Playoff Games

All conduct policies that apply to the regular season apply during All-Star,
playoff, and tournament play. The league expects all participants, fans,
volunteers, and players to cheer only positively, respect each other, and refrain
from negative comments at all times, remembering the players are kids and the
coaches and umpires are volunteers (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Softball Championship
Day & BBQ; source: website playoffs-2025). The league follows the Positive
Coaching Alliance "ROOTS" standard (Respect Officials, Opponents, Teammates, Self)
for both parents and players, and applies its standard discipline policies —
including in-game dugout removal, board discipline review for fighting or injuring
others (which can result in single or multi-game suspensions), and parent/spectator
suspensions or bans reviewed by the discipline committee based on written
complaints and on-field observation (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Discipline
Expectations & Policy).
