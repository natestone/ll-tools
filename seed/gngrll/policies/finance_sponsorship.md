# Finance & Sponsorship

{{leagueName}} is a non-profit organization. Sponsorship, donations, and fundraising directly fund uniforms, equipment, field maintenance, and player development, and help keep participation affordable for local families (source: website frequently-asked-questions/sponsorship). This section documents the league's current sponsorship offerings, financial controls, and related procedures.

## 1. Sponsorship Program & Rates

The league offers several sponsorship levels each season. The following are the current published rates (source: website home/become-a-sponsor):

- **Team Sponsorship – {{amount}}.** The sponsor's company name is printed on the back of team jerseys, and their logo and website link are featured on the league website. This fee helps cover uniform costs. Sponsors indicate in the form's comment section whether they prefer a Baseball or Softball team, and may name a specific player's team (source: website home/become-a-sponsor).
- **Banner Sponsorship.** Display a 3' x 5' company banner at the league's major fields from April through October, priced by number of fields: $250.00 (one field), $300.00 (two fields), $350.00 (three fields), $400.00 (all four fields) (source: website home/become-a-sponsor).
- **Spring Training Sponsorship – {{amount}}.** Limited to a maximum of eight businesses. Spring Training is hosted at The Cages in {{townA}} for AAA and Majors baseball and softball players; every player in the league receives a shirt featuring the sponsoring businesses' logos, worn by over 150 players (source: website home/become-a-sponsor; source: website frequently-asked-questions/sponsorship).
- **All-Star Sponsor – {{amount}}.** The sponsor's logo is featured on practice shirts for each All-Star team. To select this option a business must first sponsor a regular-season team; the opportunity is limited to 20 sponsors (source: website home/become-a-sponsor).

The league also welcomes in-kind donations of materials and services (tools, grass seed, loam, gasoline, mowing, concessions supplies, repairs, etc.); prospective in-kind donors are directed to contact {{infoEmail}} (source: website home/become-a-sponsor).

### Rate History

Team sponsorship pricing has been adjusted over time by board motion. It was $250 per team in 2019 (source: email "Re: Sponsorships"). The board voted to move team sponsorship to $375 for the 2022 season by motion (Steve, seconded by Eric; carried unanimously) (source: agenda 2022-01-22). For 2024 the team sponsorship rate was set at $475 with banners at $250/$300/$350/$450 for one through four (source: email "Re: 2024 {{leagueAbbrev}} Sponsorship Form"). The current published four-field banner rate is $400 (source: website home/become-a-sponsor). Because rates change year to year, staff should confirm the current season's rates against the website and current sponsorship form before quoting a sponsor.

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Sponsorship Coordinator.** The Sponsorship Coordinator manages sponsorship outreach, tracking, and sponsor communications. The current coordinator is Eric Hanley ({{sponsorshipEmail}}, {{leaguePhone}}), a position confirmed at annual elections (source: website home/become-a-sponsor; source: agenda 2023-01-08).
- **Treasurer.** The Treasurer manages league finances, banking, and bookkeeping and is elected/voted in by the board. The role is described as a modest commitment (roughly a couple of hours per week) and requires being added to the league's bank account (source: email "RE: Treasurer"). The Treasurer is elected by board motion (e.g., Mickey Pacanza elected Treasurer by motion, seconded, carried) (source: agenda 2022-11-14).
- **Fundraising Manager.** A distinct board role responsible for fundraising activity; it appears on the annual position slate (source: agenda 2023-01-08).

## 3. Becoming a Sponsor & Payment Procedures

- Sponsors submit through the league's fundraising/donation site ({{donationPlatform}} hosted donation page) linked from the website, or via the current season sponsorship form (source: website home/become-a-sponsor; source: email "Re: 2024 {{leagueAbbrev}} Sponsorship Form").
- Payment may also be made by PayPal donation link or by mailing a check to {{leagueName}}, {{leagueMailingAddress}} (source: email "Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} Team Sponsorship").
- Sponsor logos must be supplied in a usable format. For embroidered/sewn items, highly detailed logos may not reproduce well and vendors may request simplified artwork with outlined fonts (source: email "RE: FW: Vector Logos & Team Sponsors").
- Some corporate sponsors require a signed donation form for their internal records and may specify exact naming/branding requirements (e.g., a required prefix word ahead of the entity name); the league completes these forms as requested (source: email "Re: Dunkin' NGP Management Donation Form").

## 4. Deadlines & Timing

- Sponsorships are accepted throughout the season, but to maximize exposure (particularly for Spring Training gear) sponsors are encouraged to submit as early as possible (source: website frequently-asked-questions/sponsorship).
- To allow time to order uniforms and banners, the league targets receiving sponsorships no later than mid-February (source: email "Re: 2024 {{leagueAbbrev}} Sponsorship Form"). Historically a team-sponsor cutoff has been used to align with the uniform order (source: email "Re: Sponsorships").

## 5. Team Assignment & Fulfillment

- The Sponsorship Coordinator tracks sponsors and their team/player preferences and passes assignment details to the board and uniform team. Multiple sponsors for one team or specific presentation instructions are accommodated as best possible (source: email "Re: Sponsorships").
- Teams that lack a sponsor may be covered by assigning an extra team to long-standing sponsors so all teams are outfitted; such fill-ins are a board decision (source: email "Re: Sponsorships").
- Uniform vendor and product source selection is a board decision; cost information should be brought to the board so there are no hidden costs (source: email "Re: Sponsorships"). Jersey layout has included sponsor names on the front with the league logo, and player names/numbers on the back (source: agenda 2023-01-08).
- Sponsors are recognized via jerseys, field banners, the league website, and league communications depending on level; sponsor plaques/proofs are reviewed and approved before production (source: website frequently-asked-questions/sponsorship; source: email "Re: Approval for Sponsor Plaque layouts").

## 6. Refunds

If a sponsored player does not register or the season is affected such that a sponsorship cannot be fulfilled, the sponsor may either roll the contribution to the following season or request a refund. Refunds are processed by the Treasurer, typically by check mailed to the sponsor (source: email "Re: My sponsorship contribution of {{amount}}").

## 7. Tax-Deductibility & Acknowledgement

- {{leagueAbbrev}} is a non-profit; sponsorships are tax-deductible, and the league provides the necessary documentation to sponsors (source: website frequently-asked-questions/sponsorship).
- The league maintains an Annual Sponsorship Log tracking sponsors and donation amounts across channels (checks, PayPal, Venmo, GoFundMe). Larger donors (e.g., $250+ and $500+) are highlighted for recognition, and the sponsorship list is used to send thank-you acknowledgements (source: email "Re: Sponsor List").

## 8. Donations & Fundraising

- **Fundraising rules.** Fundraising must be tied to a specific purpose (not general/undesignated funds), and children must not conduct door-to-door solicitation (source: email "Re: Fundraising Rules").
- **Donations.** The league accepts community and corporate donations (cash, checks, and gift cards) in support of teams and tournament travel; these are coordinated through the President and Sponsorship Coordinator (source: email "Re: local community related donation"). Corporate donation forms are completed as required by the donor (source: email "Re: Dunkin' NGP Management Donation Form").
- **Special fundraising campaigns.** Larger initiatives (e.g., the Intervale Fields and Recreation Area project) run through GoFundMe and dedicated project pages, promoted across league social media and by mail to members (source: email "Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} Intervale Fields Fundraiser Launch"). All-Star tournament runs have been funded through combined GoFundMe, check, and Venmo donations (source: email "Re: Sponsor List").

## 9. Budget Approval & Spending Controls

Expenditures and budgets are approved by board motion, seconded, and recorded in the meeting minutes. Documented examples of the board authorizing spending by motion include:

- Up to {{amount}} for the Little League charter and insurance for 2022 (source: agenda 2021-12-12).
- Up to {{amount}} for baseballs and a batting cage (source: agenda 2021-12-12).
- Up to {{amount}} for equipment for the 2023 season (source: agenda 2022-11-14; source: agenda 2023-01-08).
- Up to $300 for fertilizer, and $1,500 to convert a field to 50/70 dimensions (source: agenda 2022-11-14).
- Using a proposed budget as a starting point for Intervale improvements against an {{landTrust}} budget figure (~{{amount}}K) (source: agenda 2023-01-08).

**Procedure:** Proposed expenditures should be presented to the board with cost detail; a board member makes a motion (often stated as "up to $X"), another member seconds, and the motion passes on a recorded vote (source: agenda 2023-01-08; source: agenda 2021-12-12).

## 10. Financial Reporting

The Treasurer presents a financial update at board meetings, reporting account balances (e.g., TD Bank and PayPal balances), outstanding invoices, and known obligations (source: agenda 2023-01-08; source: agenda 2021-12-12). Registration pricing, which is a primary revenue source, is set by the board with a non-volunteer fee, and changes are made by motion (source: agenda 2022-01-22).
