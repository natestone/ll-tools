# Governance & Board Roles

{{leagueName}} is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors made up of community members "passionate about the integrity & success of baseball & softball in the {{townA}}, {{townB}} & {{townC}} communities" (source: website home__board-of-directors). {{leagueAbbrev}} is a chartered Little League and operates under a Constitution & By-laws that are required to be uploaded to and kept current with Little League International (LLI) (source: agenda 2024-10-20; source: agenda 2025-10-13). This section documents how the Board is structured, how officers are elected, and how the Board conducts business.

## League Structure & Charter

{{leagueAbbrev}} was formed by merging the previously separate {{communities}} leagues, which had operated independently since the 1970s; the first combined year was 2022 (source: website home__about-{{leagueSlug}}). The Board formally pursued permanent merger of {{townC}} into the league and formalization of the merger with Little League International (source: agenda 2022-11-14 "Formalize Merger"; source: agenda 2023-10-29 "{{townC}} Merger — Formalize Merger officially with LL International").

The league's Constitution & By-laws must be reviewed, updated, and uploaded to Little League International; the Board has treated this as a recurring governance obligation (source: agenda 2024-10-20; source: agenda 2025-10-13). Documentation efforts also include maintaining a Policies & Procedures page and validating the bylaws on the league website (source: agenda 2023-10-29 "Documentation Efforts — Redo & Validate Bylaws … Policies & Procedures Page").

## Board of Directors — Voting Roles

The Board is composed of elected voting officers plus non-voting staff roles (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2022-11-14). The current published slate of voting Board positions is (source: website home__board-of-directors):

- President
- League Info Officer (LIO)
- Treasurer
- Grounds Coordinator
- Equipment
- Safety Officer
- VP Tee Ball/Coach Pitch
- VP Softball
- VP Baseball
- Umpire in Chief
- VP of Research & Development
- Sponsorship Coordinator
- Fundraising Manager
- Concessions Manager
- Coaching Coordinator
- Game and Practice Scheduler

Additional Vice President roles have been created by the Board as divisions and needs evolve — for example a VP Challenger, VP Sandlot, Secretary, and Player Agent (softball and baseball) positions have appeared on election slates (source: agenda 2024-10-20; source: agenda 2025-10-13; source: agenda 2022-11-14). Positions may be added by Board motion; for example, the Board voted to "create the position of Challenger VP in charge of the Challenger division" (source: agenda 2024-10-20).

Not all seats need be filled at any given time. Positions are routinely listed as "Open" when no volunteer has been elected (e.g., VP Baseball, Fundraising Manager, Concessions Manager, and Game and Practice Scheduler have all appeared as Open) (source: website home__board-of-directors). The league actively recruits community members to fill vacant and new roles throughout the year (source: website home__board-of-directors).

## Non-Voting Staff Roles

The Board designates non-voting staff roles that carry operational responsibility but no vote. Published non-voting roles include (source: website home__board-of-directors):

- Photo Staff
- Uniform Manager
- Uniform Team
- Background Check Officer
- LIO Staff

Additional non-voting staff (e.g., Grounds Crew) have appeared on Board slates as needed (source: agenda 2023-10-29). The Background Check Officer is a designated non-voting role responsible for volunteer background screening (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2024-10-20).

## Elections & Terms

Officers are elected annually at the fall/winter organizational Board meeting (source: agenda 2022-01-22; source: agenda 2022-11-14; source: agenda 2024-10-20). Elections are conducted by motion and second, with the outcome recorded in the meeting minutes. For example, "Motion to elect Mickey Pacanza as Treasurer by Eric Hanley. 2nd by Amanda Buxton. Motion carried unopposed." (source: agenda 2022-11-14). Newly created positions are filled the same way — the Board moved to create the Challenger VP role and then, in a separate motion, elected a person to fill it (source: agenda 2024-10-20).

When an officer does not intend to continue, they are asked to notify the President before the organizational meeting so the seat can be listed as open and recruited to the wider league. The President surveys members on which roles they will not continue in, then announces the open positions and invites the league to the meeting to fill them (source: email Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - Board Meeting Next Monday). Vacancies that arise mid-year (for example, Treasurer, Vice President, Equipment Manager, Sponsorship Manager, and Safety Officer being open at once) are filled at the next Board meeting (source: email Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - Board Meeting Next Monday).

## Board Meetings & Cadence

The Board meets on a regular schedule that shifts with the season — meeting either monthly or bi-weekly depending on the time of year in order to maximize the quality of the program (source: website home__board-of-directors). During the busy pre-season and post-season planning periods the Board schedules meetings roughly every two weeks; the Board polls members to choose meeting dates that work best (source: agenda 2022-11-14 "November/December Meetings — Choose Schedule … Amanda will poll").

Meetings are facilitated and recorded (minuted); the President typically serves as facilitator and recorder, and attendees are listed in the minutes (source: agenda 2022-01-22; source: agenda 2024-10-20). Meetings may be held in person (e.g., the middle school library) or remotely by video/phone conference when needed (source: email Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - Board Meeting Next Monday).

## Motions & Voting Procedure

Board decisions are made by motion, second, and vote, and are recorded in the minutes. The standard pattern is a named motion, a named second, and a recorded outcome — for example "Motion to move to {{amount}} for team sponsorship by Steve. Second by Eric. Motion carried unanimously." (source: agenda 2022-01-22). Outcomes are recorded as carrying "unanimously," "unopposed," or "by unanimous decision" (source: agenda 2022-01-22; source: agenda 2022-11-14; source: agenda 2024-10-20).

Motions are used both for routine business and for material spending and property decisions. Examples of Board authorizations by motion include: raising the non-volunteer fee to $40 (source: agenda 2022-01-22); authorizing up to $5,000 in equipment purchases for the season (source: agenda 2022-11-14); authorizing a batting-cage installation "not to exceed $6,500" with a named point person (source: agenda 2025-10-13); and covering the cost difference for state-championship rings (source: agenda 2024-10-20).

When a time-sensitive decision cannot wait for a scheduled meeting, the Board may conduct a vote by email, with members replying "yes" to approve. This method has been used for material actions such as authorizing the league attorney to proceed with a property purchase (source: email Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - Board meeting by email).

## Roles & Responsibilities (Selected)

- **President** — Leads the league, sets and facilitates Board meetings, records/oversees minutes, announces open positions, recruits volunteers, and serves as the league's District ({{district}}) representative (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2024-10-20 "Nate - DA {{district}}"; source: email Re: {{leagueAbbrev}} - Board Meeting Next Monday).
- **Treasurer** — Maintains league finances and presents the Treasury/P&L report and account balances at Board meetings (source: agenda 2024-10-20 "Treasury Report — P&L Statement — Balances"; source: agenda 2022-11-14 "Financial Update — Balances").
- **League Info Officer (LIO)** — Owns league communications, registration information, and the website/info systems, supported by LIO Staff (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2022-11-14).
- **Safety Officer** — Owns the ASAP (A Safety Awareness Program) safety plan and safety compliance (source: agenda 2022-11-14 "ASAP Plan Update — Melissa B, Amanda B, Info Team"; source: agenda 2025-10-13 "ASAP Plan & AED Devices").
- **Background Check Officer** — Manages volunteer background screening (non-voting) (source: website home__board-of-directors).
- **Coaching Coordinator** — Coordinates coach recruitment and training compliance (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2025-10-13).
- **Division Vice Presidents (Tee Ball/Coach Pitch, Softball, Baseball, Challenger, Sandlot, etc.)** — Oversee their division's operations, coaches, and scheduling needs (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2025-10-13).
- **Grounds Coordinator / Equipment** — Oversee field maintenance, equipment inventory, and facility work (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2024-10-20 "Equipment Inventory").
- **Sponsorship Coordinator, Fundraising Manager, Concessions Manager, Umpire in Chief, Game and Practice Scheduler, VP of Research & Development** — Own their respective functional areas as listed on the Board roster (source: website home__board-of-directors).

## Board Member Certification Requirements

All Board members must complete Little League Abuse Awareness Training, which resets each October 1 (source: agenda 2024-10-20; source: agenda 2025-10-13). The Board tracks this requirement annually as part of the fall organizational cycle (source: agenda 2024-10-20 "Abuse Awareness Training — Resets October 1 — All board members must take AA training"). Volunteers who work directly with players are also subject to background checks administered by the Background Check Officer (source: website home__board-of-directors; source: agenda 2022-01-22 "Background Check — Enforce via system if possible").

## Discipline Authority of the Board

The Board of Directors holds authority to review and act on serious conduct matters. Fighting, injuring other players, or acts beyond the scope of in-game discipline can result in immediate removal from a game and a discipline review by the {{leagueAbbrev}} Board of Directors, which can result in single- or multi-game suspensions or reinstatement to the team (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Discipline Expectations & Policy). For parent/spectator conduct, the league's practice is to review interactions after a written complaint is submitted to a league official, dispatch a representative to observe games, and refer continued issues to the discipline committee, which reviews all complaints and on-field observations and implements a discipline plan; the Board may suspend or ban parents/spectators from attending games if necessary (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} - Discipline Expectations & Policy). A standing Discipline (and Managers) committee is a recognized Board body (source: agenda 2016-12-06 "Discipline and Managers committee selection").

## Committees

The Board operates through committees and working groups for focused efforts, including a Discipline/Managers committee (source: agenda 2016-12-06), an Intervale Fields committee for the {{townB}} field project (source: email {{leagueAbbrev}} Intervale Fields Committee Meeting; source: agenda 2022-11-14), and a Development committee (source: agenda 2025-10-13 "Development committee meeting"). Committee recommendations and spending are brought back to the full Board for approval by motion (source: agenda 2022-11-14; source: agenda 2025-10-13).
